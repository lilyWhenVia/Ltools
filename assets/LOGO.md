# Ltools 标志

使用内置 imagegen 生成，透明背景的靛蓝圆角方块、白色 L 与向前箭头。设计用于桌面、程序、托盘、安装包和内置工具候选。

生成提示：Single polished Windows utility icon for Ltools. Transparent background outside one rounded-square deep indigo tile, subtle blue-violet shading, a bold white geometric capital L whose short bottom stroke becomes a forward-pointing chevron. Minimal professional flat design, legible at 16 and 32 pixels, generous padding. No words, no mockup, no watermark or external cast shadow.

Ltools.png 为生成的原始资产，Ltools.ico 由 scripts/IconBuilder.cs 导出，含 16、24、32、48、64、128、256 像素尺寸。
